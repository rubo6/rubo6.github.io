ECOBOX AI was DataLab ITAM’s computer-vision project for classifying waste. I took part as Research Manager of the community and as a member of the team: we scoped the research, trained and evaluated the model, kept the documentation in a state where new members could contribute, and delivered the trained model together with a paper (linked from my LinkedIn). The project stopped short of a deployed prototype.
